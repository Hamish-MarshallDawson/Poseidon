The Agentic IDE Challenge

Build the coding assistant that knows who it’s talking to.

01 The challenge

AI coding agents treat everyone the same. However, a student on their first day needs someone patient: someone who explains how they got there, lets them try, and checks that they followed. A senior engineer in a hurry wants the smallest correct change and no lecture. Most people sit somewhere in between, and each of them works and learns differently.

The task is to build an agentic IDE: an AI agent inside an editor that helps people debug, refactor and build real software, and adjusts to whoever is using it. That includes what it says, how much it explains, whether it acts or asks first, and how it teaches. How you do it is up to you.

02 What we’re looking for

- It works on real code. It finds the actual cause, makes fixes that hold up, and is honest about what it did.
- It reads people. It works out who it’s helping and adapts without being told.
- It’s built for someone. Besides a beginner and an engineer, pick one more persona to design for. Tell us who, why and how.
- It’s yours. Start from an open-source project or your own code, for both the editor and the agent. We score what you add.

03 How we judge


| THE MINIMUM BAR | Your agent has to run headless with one command,   agent . On a project you haven’t seen, it must cleanly fix every one of a set of simple bugs. Teams that don’t clear the bar aren’t ranked. |
|---|---|
| AUTOMATED TESTS | Once submissions close, we put every agent through the same conversations, on unseen projects, with simulated users you won’t see coming. Tests check anything code can check, like whether a bug is really fixed. An AI judge assesses how the agent treated the user, and our team reviews anything it’s unsure about. |
| OUR REVIEWERS | Four InstaLILY engineers will come to your table. Show us what you built, then we try it ourselves and look at how it’s built. That visit is your demo. We’re judging the experience, your chosen persona and how well you served them, how much thought you put into your users, and the quality of your engineering. |
| THE FINAL | The three strongest teams present to everyone before we announce the winners. |
