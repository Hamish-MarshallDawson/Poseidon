# Wordsmith: HackTheBurgh sponsor deck

## Slide 1: What is Wordsmith?

We are an **AI platform for in-house legal teams.**

The legal front door that does the work.

Used by legal teams at: Revolut · Trustpilot · Financial Times · BT · Canva · Deliveroo

## Slide 2: Who we are

We're all engineers.

| Name | Role | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Ash | Operations Engineer | Builds the systems and automations that keep Wordsmith running. |
| Eli | Product Engineer | Builds the features lawyers use in the Wordsmith platform. |
| Chapman | GTM Engineer | Builds the tools that take Wordsmith to market (GTM = go-to-market). |

## Slide 3: The challenge

**Make the most complicated end-to-end agentic AI browser-use workflow.**

Does it have to be legal focused? Ideally legally involved, but not required. Creativity required.

## Slide 4: How we judge

- **Main criterion: workflow complexity.** How complex a workflow your team managed to make.
- **Also considered:**
  - Tokens used
  - Model used
  - Time to complete the task

## Slide 5: Prizes

- **1st place:** AirPods Max, for each team member.
- **2nd and 3rd place:** Wordsmith goodie bags.
