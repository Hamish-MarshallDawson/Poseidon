## Build

1. **`src/motion/`**: the shared animation system. Decide between hand-rolled springs on `requestAnimationFrame` (position/scale/opacity only, `transform: translate3d`) and the `motion` library (`motion/react`, justify gz size); either way export `useSpring`, `usePresence`/`AnimatePresence`-style mount/unmount, `useDrag` (pointer capture, inertia-free, spring settle), and a `reducedMotion()` guard that turns durations to 0. Nothing animates `top/left/width/height`.
2. **Ship windows (`src/windows/`)**: `WindowLayer` renders `ui.windows`. Each `ShipWindow`: chrome (6-dot drag handle, letter badge in `--dvm-ship-selected` with `--dvm-text-on-accent`, name SemiBold + type, minimise and close), body = `ShipCard` (No AIS / Active states; fields from `vessels.vessels.get(mmsi)`, the `detail` reply via `adapter.send({type:'detail',mmsi})`, open gap from `vessels.gaps`, risk from `risk_p`/`risk_rank`; "AS OF" chip when `ui.feed.mode === 'replay'`). Open with a spring scale/fade from the ship's screen position; drag with pointer capture and a lifted state (2 px `--dvm-border-strong`, `--dvm-accent-subtle` title bar); minimise to a 173×38 pill docked bottom-left above the legend toggle; close removes the window and its marks. Front window = last clicked (`ui.frontMmsi`). Max 4 open: a 5th minimises the oldest. Placement: the side of the ship with more free map, never over the ship, 24 px offset from the previous window; keep windows inside the viewport on resize. `role="dialog"`, `aria-labelledby`, focus trap off (windows are non-modal), Esc closes the front window, Tab order sensible.
3. **Leader line**: an SVG overlay (one `<svg>` for all) drawing a 1 px `--dvm-ship-selected` line from the nearest edge of the front window (and any hovered window) to its ship's current screen position (`map.project` from `useMapInstance()`, updated on map `move`/`render` and on window drag, via rAF), ending in a 5 px dot at the window. Transform-only updates.
4. **Legend (`src/panels/Legend.tsx`)**: toggle bottom-left (16 px inset, centred on the scrub bar's row), panel opens upward with an 8 px gap, compact variant per the legend report (ship states and type silhouettes, risk ramp, coverage, uncertainty grammar). Drop-in animation: translateY + opacity spring. Keyboard: the toggle is a button with `aria-expanded`.
5. **Watchlist (`src/panels/Watchlist.tsx`)**: top-right summon chip ("Ranked for review · next 6 h · N") and the 360-wide drop-in panel (rows: rank, name, type, p bar, %, reason codes; footer with the honest prospective check text and the three baselines, wording from the forecast report). Rows come from `vessels.watchlist` (or the mock). Virtualise the list (no dependency needed: fixed row height). Click a row → `openShipWindow(mmsi)` and fly the map to it; hover a row → `ui.hoverMmsi`.
6. **Status line and map controls (`src/panels/StatusLine.tsx`, `MapControls.tsx`)**: top-left minimal status "● LIVE 14:07:12Z · 100 of 2,184 ships in view · 375 in stacks, 1,709 in cells · 3 cells without coverage" from `ui.feed` and `ui.view` (replay form when `mode === 'replay'`, per the timeline report), plus the "Illustrative data" tag when `ui.feed.isMock`; zoom buttons and scale bar on the right edge; the Layers button with the layers pop-over (lamps, "Showing N of M", switches bound to `ui.layers`); the log-out link.
7. **Focus UI (`src/panels/FocusBar.tsx`)**: when `ui.focusMmsi` is set, show "Leave focus [Esc]", the focused ship's name, and reason chips for `ui.focusRelated`; a breadcrumb when focus moved from one ship to another. Double-click a ship window's title (or a "Focus" button in the card) sets `ui.focusMmsi`.
8. `openShipWindow(mmsi)` helper in `src/windows/open.ts` (the map agent calls `ui.set` directly until integration; make yours the canonical one and keep the signature `openShipWindow(mmsi: number, at?: {x:number;y:number})`).
